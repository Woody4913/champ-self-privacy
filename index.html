Champ Self Privacy Policy

Champ Self is a personal wellness, fitness, nutrition, goal, and habit-tracking iOS application. This policy describes how Champ Self handles information when you use the app and enable connected services.

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Information Champ Self May Access

Champ Self only accesses health, wellness, and app-created information when needed to provide app features you choose to use. Depending on the features you enable, Champ Self may access information such as:

  • Steps
  • Activity
  • Sleep
  • Weight
  • Heart rate
  • HRV
  • Fitness metrics
  • Other HealthKit metrics explicitly authorized by you

Apple Health permissions are controlled by you through Apple's authorization system. Champ Self does not automatically have access to all Apple Health data.

Apple Health and HealthKit

Champ Self currently reads HealthKit information for enabled features. HealthKit remains the source of truth for Apple Health information, and access is granted only through user authorization.

Apple Health information may be used to display selected health metrics on Today, show fitness and wellness information, and calculate progress where appropriate. Champ Self does not upload Apple Health data to nutrition services such as USDA FoodData Central or Open Food Facts, and Apple Health information is not included in unrelated analytics.

WHOOP Connections

If you connect WHOOP, Champ Self may access data authorized through WHOOP OAuth, such as recovery, strain, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, SpO2, skin temperature, workout information, and body measurements.

Champ Self does not collect your WHOOP username or password. WHOOP account access occurs only after you authorize the connection. Tokens may be securely processed or stored by Champ Self's backend as necessary to maintain the connection, and WHOOP data is used only for enabled Champ Self features. You can disconnect WHOOP and/or revoke authorization through available app or WHOOP account controls.

App-Created Information

Champ Self may store information you create in the app, including goals, tasks, subtasks, completion records, workout records, diet logs, settings, preferences, and other records needed to support the features you use.

Locally created information remains on your device unless you deliberately use a feature that transmits or exports it.

Nutrition Search

Champ Self may send food-search queries to supported nutrition-data providers to help you find food information. Current or planned providers may include USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts, and other nutrition providers enabled by the app.

Champ Self does not upload Apple Health data to nutrition-search providers.

How Information Is Used

Health and wellness information is used to provide requested features, such as displaying selected health metrics on Today, showing fitness and wellness information, and calculating progress where appropriate.

Champ Self does not sell personal information. Champ Self does not sell health information. Health information is not used for advertising and is not shared with advertisers.

Third-Party Services

Champ Self may use third-party services only to provide requested functionality, such as health-device integrations, nutrition search, and cloud or backend services required for secure integrations. Data may be transmitted to service providers only when necessary to perform a feature you intentionally use. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Retention

App-created records are retained until deleted or reset according to app functionality. Connected-service credentials or tokens are retained only as needed to maintain the integration. Connection credentials should be removed or revoked when you disconnect the related service.

Your Choices

You can control your information in several ways:

  • Change Apple Health permissions in iOS settings.
  • Choose which health metrics are displayed in Champ Self, where supported.
  • Disconnect WHOOP and/or revoke WHOOP access.
  • Delete app-created records where available.
  • Remove Champ Self and local app data from your device.

Security

Champ Self uses reasonable technical safeguards and works to minimize sensitive-data exposure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children's Privacy

Champ Self is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

This policy may change as Champ Self evolves. When the policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
